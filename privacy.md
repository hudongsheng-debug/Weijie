# 未结 Weijie 隐私政策

适用版本 1.26.1

## 简体中文

### 保存的数据

未结将你输入的账单、账户名称、资产与负债、预算、报销、项目、预期付款和优惠券保存在设备上。如果为日常账本开启 iCloud 同步，账本数据会保存到你 Apple 账户的私有 iCloud 空间，用于在同一 Apple 账户的设备间同步。开发者没有用于接收账本的独立服务器，App 内没有广告或第三方分析 SDK。

### 权限与数据共享

删除资产或负债时，iOS 会验证设备所有者身份，未结只接收验证结果。主屏幕小组件会在同一台设备上读取月度概览和待办。导出 CSV 时，由你选择保存位置或分享对象。检查更新会向 Apple 查询 App Store 的公开版本。Apple 提供的 iCloud 和 App Store 服务适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

### 保留、删除与退出同步

本机记录会保留到你删除记录或清除应用数据。开启同步时，账本中的删除也会同步。你可在设置中关闭同步，之后的修改将不再同步，已有的 iCloud 副本仍会保留。导出的文件由你管理。

### 联系方式

如有隐私问题或希望了解数据删除方式，请通过【待填写的公开支持邮箱或联系页面】联系。

## English

### Data stored by Weijie

Weijie stores the transactions, account names, assets and liabilities, budgets, reimbursements, projects, scheduled payments, and coupons you enter on your device. If you enable iCloud sync for the Everyday Ledger, the app stores a copy in your Apple Account’s private iCloud storage to sync across devices signed in to that account. The developer operates no separate server for receiving your ledger. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

### Permissions and sharing

iOS authenticates the device owner when you delete an asset or liability. Weijie receives only the result. The Home Screen widget reads the monthly overview and upcoming tasks on the same device. When you export a CSV file, you choose where to save it or whom to share it with. Checking for updates asks Apple for the public App Store version. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its iCloud and App Store services.

### Retention, deletion, and turning off sync

Local data is kept until you delete individual records or clear app data. When sync is enabled, deletions in the ledger are synced. You can turn off sync in Settings to stop future syncing. The existing iCloud copy remains. You manage any exported files.

### Contact

For privacy questions or help with deletion, contact [public support email or contact page to be filled in before publishing].
