# 未结 Weijie 支持

适用版本 1.26.1

## 简体中文

- **怎么快速记账？** 在「记账」页点右上角「＋」。也可以在 iPhone 主屏幕添加未结的「月度概览」小组件。轻点小号小组件，或点中号小组件里的「记一笔」。
- **怎么切换币种？** 打开「设置 → 查看币种」。该选项切换当前查看的账目币种，各币种金额分别显示。
- **预算为什么变了？** 预算按原账单月份计算自己承担的净支出。实际到账的退款或报销会调整该月用量。点分类下面的「已用」可以查看明细。
- **怎么导出或同步？** 在「设置 → 导出 CSV」中选择导出范围。使用同一 Apple 账户的设备可在「设置 → iCloud 同步」中开启日常账本同步。
- **怎么联系支持？** 请通过【待填写的公开支持邮箱或联系页面】联系，并注明 App 版本、iOS 版本及操作步骤。请遮盖金额、账户名称等私人信息。

## English

- **How do I add a transaction quickly?** Open Ledger and tap +. From the iPhone Home Screen, add Weijie’s Monthly Overview widget. Tap the small widget, or tap New Entry on the medium widget.
- **How do I switch currencies?** Open Settings → Display Currency. Records and totals are shown separately for each currency.
- **Why did my budget usage change?** Weijie counts your share of an expense in the original transaction month. Refunds and reimbursements adjust that month’s usage once received. Tap the amount used under a category to see the breakdown.
- **How do I export or sync?** Open Settings → Export CSV and choose what to export. You can enable iCloud sync for the Everyday Ledger in Settings on devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
- **How do I get support?** Contact [public support email or contact page to be filled in before publishing]. Include your app version, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Redact sensitive figures and account names.
